- **Review:** a fresh reviewer, who found no security problems, flagged two things I fixed.
  - Letters NFKD can't reduce, and invisible characters, split words, so `"Straße"` gave `"stra-e"`. They are now dropped, so it gives `"strae"`.
  - Two tests proved nothing, so I replaced one and removed the other.
- **Deferred:**
  - The 200-character limit is checked on the raw input. NFKD can expand some characters, such as `㎯`, so a slug can come out longer than 200.
